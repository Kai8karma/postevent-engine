## Description
ACME Revenue Cloud VP Marketing Priya Nair hosts Northwind Analytics Head of Demand Gen Daniel Kim and Meridian Software RevOps Lead Sara Alvarez for a no-fluff breakdown of what actually happens after a webinar ends — and why most teams leave pipeline on the table.

You'll learn: (1) Why follow-up within 4 hours drives a 3.2x reply rate vs next-day, and how cutting internal routing from 18 hours to under 2 hours lifted opportunity creation 41% (11:30).
